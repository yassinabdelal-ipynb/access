Reading groups

Our reading groups meet on Tuesday and Thursday evenings in the quiet room. Each group chooses one book a month and talks about it over tea. New members are always welcome, and you do not need to have finished the book. Bring a friend if you like, or come on your own and meet new people.

The children's group meets on Saturday mornings with stories, songs and crafts for ages four to seven. Parents and carers stay with their children.
