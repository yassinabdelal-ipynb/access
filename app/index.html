Three tracks, one engine.

Focus

ADHD, anxiety, fatigue

  • The page becomes slides: about 50 words under a heading, a picture with its description, a form kept together, a table or chart on its own.
  • A progress bar, minutes left, a Sections menu and arrow keys.
  • A reading line that dims everything except a band at the pointer.
  • Optional AI plan: Claude can mark the main content, tuck away distractions (never charts, tables or code), fold side content, add headings and one-line gists.

Motor impairment

Head or eye control, limited fine motor skill

  • Head control with a 3-second right-eye wink or dwell to click.
  • Whole page with larger text and targets, or big tiles and short steps with nothing to scroll.
  • On-screen keyboard, scroll zones that speed up toward the edge, option cards, safe-first confirmations, a Rest mode.
  • A live face mask shows what the tracker sees. No camera picture is shown or sent.

Screen reader

Blind and low-vision users

  • Real landmarks (banner, navigation, search, main, footer), one h1, headings with no skipped levels and skip links.
  • Links with a clear purpose, named icon buttons, real tables with captions and column headers.
  • Optional image descriptions written by Claude's vision, marked as automatic.

Built for head control.

  • One head only. It follows the nearest head first, then that same head. Someone walking in, even nearer, never takes over.
  • Runs in the background. No window opens when tracking starts.
  • Dwell click. A ring fills at the pointer. Hovering alone never acts, and a click or wink replaces a running dwell.
  • Big tiles. Menu and tiny sections are merged, every tile says what is inside, and Claude can write clearer titles.
  • Short steps. About one field or two buttons at a time. After a button runs, you are taken to the result.
  • Keyboard and scrolling. A large on-screen keyboard that never types on hover, and scroll zones that get faster toward the screen edge.
  • Safe by default. Option cards instead of tiny dropdowns, "No, go back" first in every confirmation, Rest to pause everything.

How it works.

  1. Scan

    The page is read into plain data: text with its links, pictures and charts, tables, fields, buttons, landmarks and the site's look.

  2. Design

    A header, hero, reading column, side rail and footer are drawn in the site's colours. Each track then adapts it.

  3. Plan (optional)

    With your consent, Claude labels what each stretch is for and names tiles. It can only choose among fixed designs and cannot reword anything.

  4. Act

    Typing, clicks and submits go straight to the original page, which stays alive underneath. Close Access and it is exactly as you left it.

AI and privacy.

What Claude may do

  • Mark the main content and name what each stretch is for.
  • Hide distractions that you can bring back, and fold side content.
  • Add short headings and one-line summaries of long text.
  • Write titles and subtitles for the big tiles.
  • Describe pictures for screen readers.

It can never touch fields or submit buttons, and each rulebook is a plain file in server/policies.

What stays private

  • The key lives only in a local .env and never in the extension.
  • Field values and passwords are never sent, only short labels and snippets, and only after you agree.
  • Camera pictures stay inside the head-mouse program. Only landmark numbers reach the page.
  • The Apify agent, if you switch it on, gets only the page's web address without anything after a ? or #, only after you agree, and only for public sites.
  • The server only answers this computer, and AI plans are limited to 40 an hour.

Install.

The extension runs in Chrome or Edge. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, then Load unpacked.
  3. Choose the extension folder inside the Access folder you downloaded.
  4. Open any site, click the Access icon, then Redesign page.

Optional parts

  • AI: put your Anthropic key in .env and run node server.mjs.
  • Head control: start the same server. It launches the head-mouse program, which needs a webcam and its Python environment.
  • Apify agent: put an Apify token in .env as APIFY_TOKEN. The AI redesign button then first reads the site's hidden menu and main pages with Apify, and Claude plans the redesign with that site map.
  • Without them the redesign, Focus, and Screen reader structure still work.

Checked, and what is not.

Checked

  • 15 automated suites cover the page scan, every track, the toolbar, the tiles, the head-control bridge, the one-head rules, the Apify agent and the design tokens.
  • The real extension runs in Edge against real sites, including one live Claude call per feature.
  • Camera tracking and the face mask were checked on a live camera.

Not yet

  • Real screen readers and phones.
  • Two real people in front of the camera. The one-head rules are tested with simulated faces.
  • Charts that only draw after you interact, canvases from other sites and script-drawn widgets show as a card back to the original.
  • Gaze is research only. Head pointer with wink or dwell is the working input.
